# $2,000 Experiment Protocol

The experiment asks whether `SWING_V1.0` demonstrates repeatable positive expectancy after benchmark movement and operating costs. It does not target a profit amount or minimum trade count.

Stage A uses historical train/validation/out-of-sample and walk-forward tests across distinct regimes. Stage B uses paper execution and reconciles every order, fill, stop, journal record, and postmortem. Stage C may use a dedicated roughly $2,000 account only after the paper-to-live checklist and explicit human approval.

For trades 1–20, measure behavior: zero major risk violations, duplicates, intentional stop widening, or unauthorized instruments. For 21–50, assess expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, setup/regime results, benchmark excess return, and costs. For 51–100, compare the first and second halves to determine whether selection and error rates improved.

Potential graduation from $2,000 to $5,000 requires roughly 50–100 trades, positive expectancy, preferably profit factor above 1.5, average winner above 1.5 times average loser, drawdown below 8%, zero major control failures, positive net-of-cost results, and stability beyond one bull regime. `swing-trader report graduation` reports evidence but never transfers or increases capital.
